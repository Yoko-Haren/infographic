What exactly
is AI ?

AI is technology that lets machines imitate human intelligence: they learn from data, spot patterns, and make decisions.

Positive impacts
of AI in
daily life

Virtual assistants answer our questions in seconds
Smart product suggestions when we shop online
Face recognition keeps our phones secure
Chatbots give fast, 24/7 customer service
Handles repetitive tasks so we can focus on creativity

How can we
use AI wisely?

Be creative and stay aware. Use AI in positive ways, protect your data and privacy, and keep learning. Watch out for misuse, deepfakes, and cyberbullying. Remember: AI is a friend, not a foe!

Key terms

Machine learning
Computers learn patterns from data instead of fixed rules.
Neural network
Layers of connected “neurons” inspired by the brain.
Generative AI
AI that creates new text, images, music, or code.
