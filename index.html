Navigating AI-Powered Learning

Artificial Intelligence in Education

Introduction

In the Philippines, AI tools like chatbots and writing assistants are changing how students learn. Used wisely, AI makes studying faster and more personal, but it should support our thinking, not replace it.

Benefits

  1. 1
    Personalized learningLessons adapt to each student's own pace.
  2. 2
    Instant help, 24/7Explanations and feedback, even after class.
  3. 3
    Learning for everyoneTranslation, read-aloud and captions for all.

Risks

  1. 1
    Over-relianceCopying AI answers instead of really learning.
  2. 2
    Wrong answersAI can sound sure, yet be biased or wrong.
  3. 3
    Data privacyPersonal info typed into AI may be stored.

Responsible Use of AI in Education

1
Think first, then askUse AI to explain and check, not to do your work for you.
2
Verify & discloseFact-check AI answers and cite your AI use, as DepEd requires.
3
Protect your privacyNever share personal or sensitive information with AI tools.
